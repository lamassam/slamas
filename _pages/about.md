Hello! Thanks for visiting my site. 

I’m a research and data science professional with 8+ years of experience leading end‑to‑end projects, from defining the problem and designing rigorous studies to analyzing data, delivering insights to stakeholders, and communicating results in accessible, compelling ways.

I specialize in behavioral research, survey methodology, experimentation, and applied data science, and I have a strong track record of turning complex findings into clear, actionable insights for both technical and non‑technical audiences. I’m also an experienced presenter, writer, and cross‑functional collaborator who thrives at the intersection of data, human behavior, and real‑world impact. 

I’m passionate about digging into data to uncover insights that actually make a difference in the real world. Whether it’s mastering a new analysis technique, taking on fresh coursework, or diving deep into academic literature, I’m always eager to learn whatever I need to get to the heart of a problem. I love the challenge of unraveling complexity and translating it into clear, meaningful direction.

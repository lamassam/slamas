excerpt: 'The paper shows that when investors are asked to list their goals on the spot, they often rely on top‑of‑mind answers and miss objectives that actually matter to them, but a simple “master list” of common goals helps correct these cognitive blind spots. In a nationally representative study, 26% of participants changed their top goal after reviewing the master list, revealing how easily initial goals can shift once people see broader options. A second experiment found that a sophisticated prioritization technique (MaxDiff) produced nearly the same rankings as simple direct ranking, suggesting that straightforward ranking methods are sufficient for helping investors prioritize goals. Awarded the 2020 Montgomery-Warshauer Award.'
